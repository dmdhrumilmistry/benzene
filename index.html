About Benzene

Benzene is a free, open-source chemical structure editor that runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you draw leaves your computer (except optional PubChem name lookups).

Features: skeletal drawing, rings & templates, stereo bonds, reaction & mechanism arrows, SMILES/MOL import-export, molecular properties, isotope patterns and empirical 1H/13C NMR prediction.

NMR shifts are estimates from additive increment rules and are meant for teaching and quick sanity checks, not as a substitute for measured data.
